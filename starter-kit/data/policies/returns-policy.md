# Northpine Outfitters: Returns and Warranty Policy

Version 3.2. Valid from 1 September 2026. Northpine Outfitters is a fictional company used for training.

## 1. Change-of-mind returns

- You can return an unused product within 30 days of delivery.
- The product must be clean, complete and in its original packaging.
- Return shipping for a change-of-mind return costs 99 NOK. We deduct it from the refund.
- Sleeping bags, base layers and water filters can only be returned if they are unused and the seal is unbroken.
- Gas canisters and fuel cannot be returned.

## 2. Defective or damaged products

- Report a defect as soon as you find it. Send your order number and the receipt.
- Send at least one clear photo of the damage. A short video helps for moving parts, such as tent poles, zips and stove valves.
- Return shipping is free for defective products.
- We repair the product if a repair is possible within 14 days. If not, we replace it or refund it.

## 3. Refund approval

- A refund of 1,500 NOK or less is approved automatically when the receipt matches the order record.
- A refund above 1,500 NOK must be approved by a member of staff.
- A refund is never approved when the receipt total does not match the order record. Staff must check these cases.
- Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 working days of approval.

## 4. Warranty

- Every product has a Northpine warranty of 1 to 5 years. The warranty period is shown on the product page.
- The warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship.
- The warranty does not cover normal wear, wrong use or damage from accidents.

## 5. Damage that is not covered

- Burn holes or melted fabric from using a stove inside or too close to a tent.
- Holes in tent floors, mats or clothing made by crampons or ski edges.
- Broken tent poles when the tent was pitched in wind above its stated limit.
- Down products washed with normal detergent.
- Boots worn with a crampon type that does not match the boot rating.

## 6. Exchanges

- You can exchange a product for another size within 30 days. The first size exchange is free.

## 7. How to start a return

1. Open the Returns page in Northpine Basecamp, or write to returns@northpine.example.
2. Give your order number.
3. Upload the receipt and any photos or video.
4. You get an answer within 2 working days.
